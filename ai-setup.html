WhatsApp setup

Please read this section carefully before answering — it's the part coaches most often get caught out by.

About the number

Your WhatsApp AI runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform. A number connected to that platform is used by the system rather than through the normal WhatsApp app, so it should be a number you're not relying on for everyday personal chats. Existing chat history from another number isn't carried over.

WhatsApp also allows businesses to reply freely for 24 hours after a person's last message. Outside that window, messaging is limited by WhatsApp's own rules, so follow-ups are timed to work within them.
