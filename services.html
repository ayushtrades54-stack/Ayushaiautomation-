Everything Built Inside Your DM Automation System

This isn't a list of features. It's a complete sales machine built inside your Instagram and WhatsApp — designed to attract leads, qualify them, follow up automatically, and put serious buyers on your calendar.

We build your system in three stages: Attract → Qualify → Convert. Each component below is part of that complete journey — not a disconnected tool, but one connected funnel that runs 24/7 without you.

Stage 1 — Attract

🎯 Comment → DM Automation

That reel you posted at 11 PM? By morning, every person who commented has already received a personalised DM, been engaged with a qualifying question, and the serious ones have your booking link. You wake up with leads — without touching your phone. Most coaches post great content and then manually DM commenters one by one. This captures every single one automatically, instantly, every time.

📸 Story Reply Automation

Story replies are one of the most underused lead sources for fitness coaches. Someone who reacts to your story is already warmed up — they know you, they like your content, and they just raised their hand. The system catches every reaction and reply, starts a qualifying conversation immediately, and moves genuine interest toward a booking. Coaches using this report capturing leads they would have completely missed before.

Stage 2 — Qualify

📱 Instagram DM Automation

Every DM you receive — whether someone says "Hi", asks about pricing, or reaches out after seeing your reel — gets an instant, intelligent reply in under 60 seconds. Not a generic auto-reply. A personalised conversation that asks smart questions about their goal, timeline, and readiness. The system filters time-wasters automatically and pushes only serious prospects forward. You never reply to the same FAQ twice again.

🔍 Lead Qualification System

Every lead that enters your DMs gets asked three things: what's their goal, when do they want to start, and are they ready to invest in themselves. Based on their answers, the system tags them automatically — fat loss, muscle gain, beginner, ready buyer, not yet ready. When you open your DMs, you immediately know who to focus on and who needs more time. No more guessing who's serious.

💬 WhatsApp Automation

The highest-intent leads from Instagram get moved to WhatsApp for a deeper qualification sequence. WhatsApp has a higher open and response rate than Instagram DMs for serious conversations — and our flows are built to take advantage of that. Leads get pre-qualified further, objections get handled, and only genuinely interested, financially-ready prospects make it through to your calendar.

Stage 3 — Convert

📅 Automated Booking Funnel

Your booking link only goes out after a lead has been qualified. That means every single call on your calendar is with someone who already knows your offer, has told you their goal, and is ready to invest. No more taking discovery calls with people who were never going to buy. Your time on the phone becomes 10x more productive — and your close rate goes up.

⚡ Hot Lead Alert System

When someone enters your funnel showing all the signs of high intent — fast replies, strong goal clarity, budget readiness — you get an instant real-time notification. You can jump in personally and close them while they're at peak interest. Some of the biggest coaching deals happen in the first 10 minutes. This makes sure you're there for those moments, every time.

🔁 Follow-Up & Re-Engagement System

Leads don't stop being valuable the moment they go quiet. The system sends smart follow-ups at 1 hour, 24 hours, and 3 days — each with a slightly different angle to re-open the conversation. It also runs re-engagement sequences for older leads who went cold weeks ago. The coaches who run this recover 2–4 leads per month that would have been permanently lost without it.
