What Gets Built Inside Your System

Two categories, doing two different jobs. Managed Automation answers reliably using structured flows. AI Agent Automation holds a real conversation around your coaching business — and decides who's worth your time.

Managed Automation

1 · Rule-Based Automation

Structured flows, built and managed

Keywords, buttons and predefined paths on established automation platforms such as ManyChat. For a coach whose DMs are mostly the same handful of questions — price, does it include a diet plan, do I need a gym — this handles them perfectly and consistently at the lowest cost. It follows the route it was given rather than reasoning about what a lead just wrote.

AI Agent Automation

2 · AI Agent

A conversation, not a decision tree

An agent configured around your programs, pricing, tone and qualification process. A lead writes “I've tried online coaching before and it didn't stick, is yours different?” — no keyword matches that, but the agent answers it using your own words, remembers they said it, and factors it into how the rest of the conversation goes. That is the difference you are paying for.

AI Agent Automation

3 · What the Agent Handles

Availability varies by product. Each capability below notes where it applies.

Stage 1 — Attract

Comment → DM

Included with every Instagram product

Someone comments your keyword under a reel or post. They get a public reply that keeps the post looking active, and a DM that opens the conversation privately. From there the agent takes over — answering, or qualifying, depending on your product.

Included at no extra cost on Basic, Pro, Business, Fusion and Fusion Max. It is never sold as a paid add-on.

Always-on enquiry handling

All products

A DM at 2am gets the same answer as one at 2pm. The agent replies automatically, usually within seconds, using your programs, pricing and policies — never invented information. If it hasn't been given an answer, it says you'll confirm.

Stage 2 — Qualify

Lead qualification

Pro · Business · WhatsApp PA · Fusion · Fusion Max

The questions are yours — goal, timeline, readiness, budget, whatever matters for your offer. They're asked one at a time, in your order, never dumped as a list. Once a lead answers, that answer is locked and never asked again, even if the conversation wanders.

The agent asks permission once before starting, so it doesn't feel like an interrogation. Messy, slang and out-of-order answers are understood.

Context that carries

All products

The agent remembers the recent conversation, picks up the lead's name naturally, and won't repeat something it already told them. Higher tiers handle rapid-fire messages as one thought rather than firing back three separate replies.

Voice notes

Business · Fusion Max only

Leads who'd rather talk than type get understood too. Voice messages are transcribed and answered like any typed message, and count toward qualification the same way.

This is a higher-tier capability. It is not included in Basic, Pro, WhatsApp Personal Assistant or Fusion.

Human handoff

All products

If a lead asks for a real person, the agent stops replying in that conversation and leaves it with you. Any follow-ups it had queued for that lead are cancelled, so nobody gets an automated nudge in the middle of a conversation with you.

4 · Convert

Booking-link delivery

Pro · Business · WhatsApp PA · Fusion · Fusion Max

Your booking destination — the link you give during setup — goes out once, only after a lead qualifies, and only one time. Nobody who already booked gets pestered with the link again.

To be precise about what this is: the agent sends your link. It does not read, write to, or manage your calendar, and it does not check real-time availability.

Follow-ups that stop when they should

Pro · Business · WhatsApp PA · Fusion · Fusion Max

Leads who go quiet get a nudge matched to where the conversation stopped. The moment they reply, book, or get handed to you, everything queued cancels itself. The agent also won't schedule a follow-up while someone is still actively typing.

Pro, WhatsApp PA and Fusion use follow-up messages you write in advance, chosen to match the lead's stage. Business and Fusion Max write each follow-up fresh, referencing that lead's actual conversation.

Cross-channel continuity

Fusion · Fusion Max

A lead qualified on Instagram moves to WhatsApp with one tap and is recognised automatically. Questions they've already answered are never asked again. Someone who messages WhatsApp directly is qualified normally from the start.

A lead record you can actually use

All products

Every conversation is logged with the qualification answers and current status, so you can see who's ready, who stalled and where. Higher tiers add a flat view you can sort, filter and export.

How these fit together: the F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework

Everything above runs inside one structure rather than as loose features. Filter — qualification separates serious buyers from browsers. Influence — while qualifying, the conversation answers real coaching objections in your words. Transfer — your booking link reaches only the leads who qualified, once.

It applies to Pro, Business, WhatsApp Personal Assistant, Fusion and Fusion Max, and to the Growth and Combo Managed plans. Basic answers enquiries accurately but does not run the full journey.

About the platform

Managed Automation is built on established automation platforms such as ManyChat. It's a capable, widely used tool and the right choice for structured, rule-based flows. Platform subscription costs for Managed Automation are separate from the service fee and are confirmed with you before setup.

Being straight about the limits

  • It doesn't message strangers. Everything starts from someone contacting or interacting with you first.
  • It doesn't manage your calendar. It sends the booking link you provide.
  • It doesn't replace you on a sales call. It gets the right people to that call.
  • It isn't flawless. Platform changes, outages and unusual messages happen — fixing those is part of the monthly service.
