Instagram · Capability level: Conversational

Basic — Instagram inbox & FAQ assistant

Basic is an Instagram DM assistant that answers enquiries from your own knowledge base — automatically, day or night, in your words. When a question falls outside what it was given, it says you'll confirm rather than guessing.

Who it's for: Coaches whose DMs are mostly the same questions — pricing, what's included, how coaching works — and who don't need qualification yet.

What it actually handles

The questions your inbox gets every day: price, what a program includes, how coaching works, who it suits. It picks up the lead's name naturally, keeps track of what has already been said so it doesn't repeat itself, turns keyword comments on your posts and reels into DMs, hands the conversation to you when someone asks for a person, and records every conversation as a lead record.

What this capability level means

Conversational. The agent understands what each lead actually wrote and answers accurately — but it doesn't run a qualification journey or manage the lead afterwards.

What makes it different: It's the accurate-answers level. It answers in your words from your knowledge base and never invents an answer, but it doesn't run a qualification journey.

How it differs from the next level up

Pro adds the entire conversion side: your qualification questions one at a time, your booking link released only to leads who qualify, follow-ups for people who go quiet, and a hot lead alert by email.

Where Basic stops

No qualification, no follow-ups, no booking-link delivery, no hot lead alerts and no voice-note understanding. Instagram only.

What it does

  • Automatic replies to Instagram DMs, day or night
  • Answers from your knowledge base — pricing, programs, policies
  • Defers to you instead of inventing answers
  • Picks up the lead's name and keeps recent context
  • Won't repeat what it already said
  • Human handoff when a lead asks for you
  • Comment → DM included
  • Every conversation logged

Not included

  • Lead qualification
  • Follow-ups
  • Booking-link delivery
  • Voice notes
  • Hot lead alerts

Instagram · Capability level: Qualifying

Pro — Instagram lead qualification AI

Pro is the Instagram lead-qualification agent and the recommended starting point. It does everything Basic does, then runs the full F.I.T. journey — Filter, Influence, Transfer — on every enquiry.

Who it's for: Coaches with steady Instagram enquiries who need serious leads identified, followed up and sent to booking. The recommended starting point.

What it actually handles

Answering from your knowledge; asking permission once and then your qualification questions one at a time, in your order; locking each answer so nobody is asked twice; understanding messy and out-of-order replies; sending your booking link once, only after a lead qualifies; template follow-ups matched to where the conversation stopped, which cancel the moment the lead replies, books or is handed to you; a hot lead alert to your email; and a full lead record you can act on.

What this capability level means

Qualifying. The agent doesn't only reply — it moves a conversation through a structure you define and decides who reaches your booking link.

What makes it different: Pro runs the full F.I.T. journey: your questions one at a time, answers locked, your booking link released only after qualification, and follow-ups for leads who go quiet. The 14-day performance guarantee applies to Pro.

How it differs from the next level up

Business adds voice-note understanding, follow-ups written fresh for each lead instead of from approved templates, rapid messages answered as one, layered reliability and cleaner, exportable lead data.

Where Pro stops

No voice notes and no AI-written follow-ups. Instagram only — WhatsApp needs WhatsApp Personal Assistant or Fusion. It sends your booking link and never reads or writes your calendar.

What it does

  • Everything in Basic
  • Your qualification questions, one at a time, in your order
  • Permission asked once before qualifying
  • Answers locked — never asked twice
  • Messy and out-of-order answers understood
  • Booking link sent once, only after qualification
  • Template follow-ups matched to the lead's stage
  • Follow-ups cancel on reply, booking or handoff
  • Hot lead alert by email
  • Full lead record

Not included

  • Voice notes
  • AI-written follow-ups

Instagram · Capability level: Contextual

Business — Advanced Instagram AI agent

Business is the advanced Instagram agent, built for audiences that would rather talk than type and for offers where a follow-up has to sound personal rather than templated.

Who it's for: Coaches with a voice-heavy audience or a higher-ticket offer, where a personal-sounding follow-up matters.

What it actually handles

Everything Pro handles, plus voice notes transcribed and answered in text — with a voice answer counting toward qualification — follow-ups written fresh for that specific lead, several rapid messages answered as one reply, cleaned lead data in a flat view that can be sorted and exported, and conversation rules that can be adjusted without a rebuild.

What this capability level means

Contextual. The agent handles the real texture of a conversation: audio, bursts of short messages, and follow-ups that reference what that person actually said.

What makes it different: Business understands voice notes and writes each follow-up fresh for that lead, with deeper context handling, layered reliability and cleaner lead data.

How it differs from the next level up

Fusion Max is this same level linked with WhatsApp, so one lead is recognised across both channels.

Where Business stops

Instagram only. AI-written follow-ups replace template follow-ups rather than running alongside them. Voice notes are understood and answered in text — no product sends voice replies — and there is no calendar connection.

What it does

  • Everything in Pro
  • Voice notes understood and answered in text
  • Voice answers count toward qualification
  • AI-written follow-ups for each lead
  • Several rapid messages answered as one
  • Layered reliability so conversations don't stall
  • Lead data cleaned before it's recorded
  • Flat lead view that can be sorted and exported
  • Conversation rules adjustable without a rebuild

WhatsApp · Capability level: Qualifying

WhatsApp Personal Assistant — WhatsApp AI assistant

The WhatsApp Personal Assistant brings Pro-level qualification to WhatsApp, for coaches whose enquiries arrive there from a link in bio, from ads, from a website or by word of mouth.

Who it's for: Coaches whose enquiries land on WhatsApp rather than Instagram.

What it actually handles

Automatic replies from your knowledge base; qualification one question at a time with answers locked; your booking link sent once after qualification; template follow-ups that cancel on reply; several short messages combined into one clear reply; layered reliability; human handoff; hot lead alerts; and a full lead record.

What this capability level means

Qualifying. The same structured journey as Pro, running on WhatsApp.

What makes it different: The qualification quality of Pro, on WhatsApp, with multi-message combining and layered reliability. It needs a dedicated WhatsApp business number.

How it differs from the next level up

Fusion adds the Instagram side and links the two channels. Fusion Max adds voice-note understanding and AI-written follow-ups on WhatsApp as well.

Where WhatsApp Personal Assistant stops

No voice notes and no AI-written follow-ups at this level, and no Instagram side, so no Comment → DM. It needs a dedicated WhatsApp business number, existing chat history doesn't carry over, and WhatsApp's 24-hour window shapes follow-up timing.

Before ordering, read about the dedicated number and the 24-hour window.

What it does

  • Automatic replies to WhatsApp enquiries
  • Answers from your knowledge base
  • Qualification one question at a time, answers locked
  • Booking link sent once after qualification
  • Template follow-ups that cancel on reply
  • Several short messages answered as one
  • Layered reliability
  • Human handoff
  • Hot lead alert by email
  • Full lead record

Not included

  • Voice notes
  • AI-written follow-ups
  • Instagram and Comment → DM

Instagram + WhatsApp · Capability level: Qualifying, cross-channel

Fusion — Instagram + WhatsApp AI system

Fusion is one connected system rather than two products: Instagram Pro and the WhatsApp Personal Assistant, linked so a lead doesn't start over when the conversation moves channel.

Who it's for: Coaches whose leads discover them on Instagram and book on WhatsApp.

What it actually handles

Everything Pro handles on Instagram and everything the WhatsApp Personal Assistant handles on WhatsApp, plus recognition of a lead who moves from Instagram to WhatsApp so the questions they already answered are never asked again. Someone who messages WhatsApp directly is qualified from the start, and each channel runs its own follow-ups.

What this capability level means

Qualifying, cross-channel. Pro-level capability on both sides, with continuity between them.

What makes it different: Fusion is Instagram Pro and WhatsApp Personal Assistant linked as one product. A lead qualified on Instagram continues on WhatsApp without repeating answers. It costs less than buying both separately, and it's actually connected.

How it differs from the next level up

Fusion Max is the same connection at Business level: voice notes and AI-written follow-ups on both channels.

Where Fusion stops

No voice notes and no AI-written follow-ups. The WhatsApp requirements still apply — a dedicated business number and the 24-hour window — and booking still happens through your own link.

What it does

  • Everything in Pro on Instagram
  • Everything in WhatsApp Personal Assistant on WhatsApp
  • Lead recognised when moving from Instagram to WhatsApp
  • Answered questions never asked again
  • Direct WhatsApp leads qualified from the start
  • Template follow-ups on each channel
  • Comment → DM included
  • Human handoff and hot lead alerts

Not included

  • Voice notes
  • AI-written follow-ups

Instagram + WhatsApp · Capability level: Contextual, cross-channel

Fusion Max — Advanced Instagram + WhatsApp AI system

Fusion Max is the complete system: the Business-level Instagram agent linked with the WhatsApp Personal Assistant, with the highest capability on both channels.

Who it's for: Coaches running both channels whose audience sends voice notes or whose offer is high-ticket. If neither is true, Fusion does the job.

What it actually handles

Everything Business handles on Instagram, everything the WhatsApp assistant handles on WhatsApp, voice notes understood on both channels, AI-written follow-ups on both, cross-channel recognition and continuity, layered reliability and an exportable lead view.

What this capability level means

Contextual, cross-channel — the highest level offered.

What makes it different: The Business-level Instagram agent linked with WhatsApp Personal Assistant: voice notes and AI-written follow-ups on both channels, with cross-channel continuity.

How it differs from the next level up

There is no level above this. If voice notes and AI-written follow-ups don't matter for your audience, Fusion does the same cross-channel job for less.

Where Fusion Max stops

Still no calendar connection, no voice replies, no cold outreach, no Messenger and no third-party integrations. It's the largest build, so the timeline is around 72 hours or more.

What it does

  • Business-level capability on Instagram
  • WhatsApp Personal Assistant on WhatsApp
  • Voice notes understood on both channels
  • AI-written follow-ups on both channels
  • Cross-channel recognition and continuity
  • Layered reliability across both
  • Flat lead view that can be exported
  • Comment → DM, human handoff and hot lead alerts
