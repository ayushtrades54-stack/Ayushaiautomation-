All six are AI Agent Automation: configured around your business, run as a managed service, and sold as a one-time setup plus a monthly fee. Instagram products include Comment → DM at no extra cost. Every product has human handoff. None connects to your calendar — qualifying products send your booking link.

What every AI Agent product shares

Whichever level you choose, the agent is configured around your business and run for you as a managed service. Every product:

  • Answers from your knowledge only. Your programs, pricing, policies and objection answers are loaded in as the agent's single source of facts. Anything outside them is deferred to you — it never fills the gap with a guess.
  • Speaks as your business. Your assistant name, your coach name, your tone.
  • Remembers the conversation. Recent history is carried into every reply, so leads aren't asked the same thing twice or greeted like strangers.
  • Steps aside for you. When a lead asks for a real person, the agent stops replying in that conversation and leaves it with you. Anything it had queued for that lead is cancelled too.
  • Records every conversation. Each lead gets a stored record and a full message log.
  • Only ever responds. Nothing is sent to anyone who hasn't messaged or commented first.

What changes as you go up the range is how much of the conversation the agent is trusted with: answering only, then qualifying and converting, then handling voice and writing its own follow-ups, then doing all of it across two channels at once.

What the agent can understand, and what it sends

It can understand

  • Typed messages, including messy, casual or out-of-order answers
  • Several short messages sent in a burst (Business, WhatsApp PA, Fusion Max)
  • Keyword comments on your posts and reels (Instagram products)
  • Voice notes — transcribed and answered in the conversation, on Business and Fusion Max only

It sends

  • Text replies, in your tone
  • Your booking link — once, after qualification (Pro and above)
  • Your follow-up messages, or follow-ups written for that lead
  • A public comment reply plus a private DM, for Comment → DM

It never sends voice notes. Voice is something the agent understands on the products that support it — every reply goes out as text, including the reply to a voice note.

On products without voice support, a voice note or an image gets a short, polite reply asking the lead to send it as text, so nothing is silently ignored.

Instagram · Capability level: Conversational

Basic — Instagram inbox & FAQ assistant

Basic is an Instagram DM assistant that answers enquiries from your own knowledge base — automatically, day or night, in your words. When a question falls outside what it was given, it says you'll confirm rather than guessing.

Who it's for: Coaches whose DMs are mostly the same questions — pricing, what's included, how coaching works — and who don't need qualification yet.

What it actually handles

The questions your inbox gets every day: price, what a program includes, how coaching works, who it suits. It picks up the lead's name naturally, keeps track of what has already been said so it doesn't repeat itself, turns keyword comments on your posts and reels into DMs, hands the conversation to you when someone asks for a person, and records every conversation as a lead record.

What this capability level means

Conversational. The agent understands what each lead actually wrote and answers accurately — but it doesn't run a qualification journey or manage the lead afterwards.

What makes it different: It's the accurate-answers level. It answers in your words from your knowledge base and never invents an answer, but it doesn't run a qualification journey.

Instagram · Capability level: Qualifying

Pro — Instagram lead qualification AI

Pro is the Instagram lead-qualification agent and the recommended starting point. It does everything Basic does, then runs the full F.I.T. journey — Filter, Influence, Transfer — on every enquiry.

Who it's for: Coaches with steady Instagram enquiries who need serious leads identified, followed up and sent to booking. The recommended starting point.

What it actually handles

Answering from your knowledge; asking permission once and then your qualification questions one at a time, in your order; locking each answer so nobody is asked twice; understanding messy and out-of-order replies; sending your booking link once, only after a lead qualifies; template follow-ups matched to where the conversation stopped, which cancel the moment the lead replies, books or is handed to you; a hot lead alert to your email; and a full lead record you can act on.

What this capability level means

Qualifying. The agent doesn't only reply — it moves a conversation through a structure you define and decides who reaches your booking link.

What makes it different: Pro runs the full F.I.T. journey: your questions one at a time, answers locked, your booking link released only after qualification, and follow-ups for leads who go quiet. The 14-day performance guarantee applies to Pro.

Business is the advanced Instagram agent, built for audiences that would rather talk than type and for offers where a follow-up has to sound personal rather than templated.

Contextual. The agent handles the real texture of a conversation: audio, bursts of short messages, and follow-ups that reference what that person actually said.

What actually changes as you move up

Each level adds a specific capability, not a vague "better AI":

  • Basic → Pro: the whole conversion layer arrives — your qualification questions asked one at a time with answers stored and never repeated, your booking link released only to qualified leads, and multi-step follow-ups for people who go quiet. This is the biggest single jump in the range.
  • Pro → Business: the agent starts handling harder conversations. It understands voice notes, writes each follow-up itself around what that lead said, tidies answers into consistent data, answers bursts of messages as one reply, and gives you a flat lead view you can sort and export.
  • Instagram → WhatsApp Personal Assistant: the same qualifying journey, on the channel where your enquiries land, with a dedicated business number and WhatsApp's own messaging rules respected.
  • Single channel → Fusion: Instagram and WhatsApp stop being two separate conversations. A lead who part-qualified on Instagram is recognised on WhatsApp and carries their answers with them.
  • Fusion → Fusion Max: the Business-level capabilities — voice notes and self-written follow-ups — run on both channels instead of one.

Higher tiers cost more because they take more of the conversation off you, not because the same system is priced differently. If a capability wouldn't change how your DMs are handled, the lower tier is the honest choice — see which product fits your situation.

Comment → DM: turning comments into conversations

Someone comments your keyword on a post or reel. Two things happen: a public reply appears under their comment, so the post keeps its activity and other people see you respond — and a private DM opens the conversation with them directly. The agent for your product then takes the conversation from there.

Different keywords for different offers

You aren't limited to one keyword. Each campaign is its own keyword with its own set of replies, so a single account can run several at once. For example:

  • "TRANSFORM" on a transformation reel → replies and a DM about your results content and how the transformation happened
  • "COACH" on a program post → replies and a DM that opens the conversation about coaching
  • "PLAN" on a free-resource post → replies and a DM delivering that resource

Each keyword is attached to its own pool of public replies and DM openers, so the wording matches the content the person commented on. A campaign can also be set to respond to every comment on your posts rather than a specific keyword, when that suits the content better.

Details worth knowing

  • Natural variation. Each campaign holds several approved replies and one is chosen at random, so ten commenters don't all receive an identical sentence.
  • It reaches non-followers. The private reply opens a DM with someone who commented even if they've never messaged you.
  • No duplicates. A comment is only ever acted on once, so nobody gets the same DM twice.
  • Your own comments don't trigger it.
  • It's included. Comment → DM is part of every Instagram AI product at no extra cost, and is edited as your campaigns change.

Comment → DM is Instagram-only, so it isn't part of WhatsApp Personal Assistant. More detail: Comment → DM on the Channels page.
