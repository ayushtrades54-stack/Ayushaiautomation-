Instagram DM automation

For most fitness coaches, Instagram is where interest turns into a conversation: a reel lands, someone comments, someone DMs "how much?" at 11pm. Instagram DM automation makes sure every one of those messages gets a prompt, accurate answer in your words — and, on the qualifying products, a conversation that works out who's ready.

The Instagram products step up in capability:

  • Basic — answers enquiries from your knowledge and hands over when asked. No qualification or follow-ups.
  • Pro — adds qualification, booking-link delivery, template follow-ups and hot lead alerts.
  • Business — adds voice notes, AI-written follow-ups, multi-message handling and deeper lead handling.

Instagram access is connected through Meta's official process. You keep ownership, and you never share a password.

Included free on every Instagram AI product

Comment → DM

A comment under a transformation reel is often your highest-intent moment. Comment → DM turns it into a private conversation straight away:

  1. Keyword commentA lead comments your chosen keyword on a post or reel.
  2. Public replyA public reply keeps the post looking active.
  3. Private DMA DM opens the conversation privately.
  4. Agent takes overThe agent answers or qualifies, depending on your product.

Your own comments never trigger it. Comment → DM is included at no extra cost on Basic, Pro, Business, Fusion and Fusion Max — it's never sold as an add-on. It isn't part of WhatsApp Personal Assistant, which has no Instagram side.

Instagram lead qualification

On the qualifying Instagram products, the agent asks permission once, then works through your questions one at a time in your order. Answers are locked once given. Messy or out-of-order replies are understood. Your booking link goes out once, only after the lead qualifies, and a hot lead alert reaches your email.

The full mechanics are in How it works → Lead qualification.

Instagram follow-ups

Leads who go quiet get a configured follow-up matched to where the conversation stopped. Pro and Fusion use template follow-ups you approve in advance. Business and Fusion Max write each follow-up fresh for that lead. Every follow-up cancels the moment the lead replies, books or is handed to you, and none is scheduled while the lead is still writing. Follow-ups stay within Instagram's own messaging rules.

WhatsApp AI automation

Some coaches' enquiries land on WhatsApp — from a link in bio, an ad, a website or word of mouth. The WhatsApp Personal Assistant brings the same qualification quality there:

  • Automatic replies from your knowledge base
  • Your qualification questions, one at a time, locked once answered
  • Your booking link, sent once after qualification
  • Template follow-ups that cancel on reply
  • Several short messages combined into one clear reply
  • Layered reliability, so conversations don't stall
  • Human handoff, a full lead record and hot lead alerts

Voice notes aren't part of WhatsApp Personal Assistant, and neither are AI-written follow-ups. Both are available on WhatsApp through Fusion Max.

The dedicated WhatsApp business number

This is the requirement coaches most often overlook, so it's settled before anything is bought. WhatsApp automation runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform, not the normal WhatsApp app. That means:

  • You need a dedicated business number that isn't used for personal chats.
  • Existing chat history doesn't carry over from another number.

The 24-hour messaging window

WhatsApp lets a business reply freely for 24 hours after a person's last message. Outside that window, messaging is limited by WhatsApp's own rules, and some messages need pre-approved templates. This shapes follow-up timing on every WhatsApp product, and it's a platform rule no provider can switch off.

Instagram + WhatsApp: Fusion and Fusion Max

Plenty of coaching funnels start on Instagram and finish on WhatsApp. If those two channels aren't connected, the lead starts over on the second one and repeats everything. Fusion and Fusion Max connect them.

Fusion is Instagram Pro and WhatsApp Personal Assistant linked as one system. Fusion Max is the Business-level Instagram agent linked with WhatsApp Personal Assistant, with voice notes and AI-written follow-ups on both channels. A lead who half-qualifies on Instagram is recognised on WhatsApp, and questions they already answered aren't asked again. Someone who messages WhatsApp directly is qualified normally from the start. Each channel runs its own follow-ups.

One product, two capability levels: Fusion and Fusion Max. Fusion costs less than buying the Instagram and WhatsApp products separately — and, unlike two separate products, it's actually connected.

Channel limitations, stated plainly

  • No cold outreach on either channel. Everything starts from the lead.
  • No calendar connection. The system sends your booking link and doesn't manage your calendar.
  • No Facebook Messenger. The products cover Instagram and WhatsApp.
  • Platform decisions sit with Meta. Everything is built within its rules, and adapting to rule changes is part of the monthly service — but nobody can promise immunity from platform decisions.
  • Occasional platform issues, delays or outages can happen. Fixing what's within the managed service is included in the monthly fee.
