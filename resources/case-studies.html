Anchal Mishra — from Growth Plan to an Instagram + WhatsApp Combo system

Anchal MishraFat loss and rehab coach

Category

Managed Automation

Plans

Growth Plan (Instagram) → Combo Plan

Channels

Instagram and WhatsApp

Lead sources

Instagram, including ads

The situation

Anchal's Instagram was generating strong inbound interest, from content and from ads. Leads came in through Instagram DMs and then moved into WhatsApp to continue the conversation. Qualification was part of the process, because not every enquiry was a fit for a fat loss and rehab coaching offer.

The problem

As volume grew, handling these conversations by hand became difficult to manage: answering the same questions, working out who was serious, and moving the right people from Instagram to WhatsApp without losing them in between.

What was built

The work started on the Growth Plan — rule-based Instagram automation with qualification, a booking funnel and follow-ups. As inbound grew and WhatsApp became central to the process, the setup moved to the Combo Plan, which adds a structured Instagram → WhatsApp handover, a booking funnel and missed-lead recovery on top of Growth.

  1. Instagram enquiryFrom content or ads.
  2. Structured repliesCommon questions answered consistently.
  3. QualificationRule-based questions identify fit.
  4. WhatsApp handoverQualified conversations move to WhatsApp.
  5. Booking funnelLeads directed to the next step.

Outcome

The Combo automation handled the inbound volume it was introduced for, and worked effectively for its operational purpose: structuring enquiries, qualifying them and moving them from Instagram to WhatsApp. No revenue, lead-count or conversion figures are published, because none are claimed.
