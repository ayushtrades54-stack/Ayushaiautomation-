The short version

What Ayush AI Automation actually builds

Ayush AI Automation designs, configures, builds and manages DM conversation systems for fitness coaches on Instagram and WhatsApp. It isn't an AI reply box bolted onto your inbox, and it isn't a platform you're handed and left to set up. Each system is configured around the coach's business and conversation requirements, then run as a managed service every month.

At a public level, this is the path every enquiry takes:

  1. Lead messageA DM, a keyword comment or a WhatsApp message arrives.
  2. UnderstandingThe system reads what the lead actually wrote.
  3. Your knowledgeIt answers from your programs, pricing, policies and objection answers.
  4. QualificationYour questions, one at a time, where the product supports it.
  5. Lead recordAnswers, status and context are stored in a structured record.
  6. Follow-upQuiet leads get a configured follow-up, where supported.
  7. TransferQualified leads get your booking link, and you get a hot lead alert.
  8. Human handoffAnyone who asks for a person gets you.

This structure follows the F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework — Filter, Influence, Transfer. Which steps run depends on the product: Basic stops at answering and handoff, and the qualifying products run the full journey. The products page maps exactly which product does what.

AI conversation handling

Rule-based automation matches keywords and buttons. That works well when every lead asks the same few things. Coaching leads usually don't: they write in full sentences, mix two questions into one message, and bring up things no keyword anticipated — "I've tried online coaching before and it didn't stick, is yours different?"

An AI Agent reads the message itself rather than scanning it for a trigger word. It works out what's being asked, answers from your knowledge, and keeps the conversation moving in the direction you've configured. Replies are automatic and usually land within seconds, though no hard response time is promised on someone else's platform.

What it handles well

Varied wording, messy or out-of-order answers, slang, questions buried inside longer messages, and the objections coaching leads actually raise.

What it deliberately doesn't do

It doesn't invent answers, run your sales call, message people who never contacted you, or pretend to know things you didn't give it.

On Business, WhatsApp Personal Assistant and Fusion Max, a lead who sends several short messages in a row gets one combined reply instead of three separate ones.

Coach knowledge and configuration

During setup, your onboarding answers become the knowledge layer the agent works from. That includes your offer and programs, what each program includes, your pricing and how you want it presented, who you do and don't work with, your most common questions and your answers, your objection answers in your own words, your tone, and the phrases you never want used.

This is why the agent sounds like your business rather than a generic assistant. It is also why it can say "I'll confirm that for you" instead of guessing when something falls outside what it was given.

Qualification questions, the point at which your booking link is released, follow-up wording and handoff behaviour are all configured here too. Changing them later is an edit, not a rebuild.

Conversation context

Every AI product keeps relevant context across recent messages. It picks up the lead's name naturally, remembers what was already covered, and won't repeat information it has already given. Higher capability plans handle context more deeply — combining rapid messages and treating voice notes as part of the same conversation.

On Fusion and Fusion Max, context carries across channels. A lead who starts on Instagram and moves to WhatsApp is recognised, and the questions they already answered aren't asked again.

Lead qualification

Qualification is the Filter stage of F.I.T. The questions are yours — goal, timeline, readiness, budget, or whatever matters for your offer. The agent asks permission once so it doesn't feel like an interrogation, then asks your questions one at a time, in your order, never as a list.

Leads rarely answer neatly. Someone might give their goal and timeline in one message, or answer question three before question two. The agent understands messy, slang and out-of-order answers and records them against the right question.

Illustrative example — not a real client conversation

hey how much is ur coaching

Hi! Happy to explain. The price depends on which program fits you best — mind if I ask a couple of quick questions first?

sure. i want to lose around 8 kg before my wedding in april

Congratulations! That gives us a clear goal and timeline. Have you worked with a coach before?

Goal and timeline recorded from one message · next question asked

Basic doesn't qualify. It answers enquiries accurately and hands over, which is exactly right when you don't yet need qualification.

Answer boundaries and answer locking

Two boundaries keep conversations clean:

  • Knowledge boundary. The agent answers only from what you supplied. Anything outside it gets deferred to you, not improvised.
  • Answer locking. On qualifying products, once a lead answers a question, that answer is locked. The agent won't ask it again, even if the conversation wanders for days.

Nobody gets asked their goal three times, and nobody gets a price you never quoted.

Lead management and structured lead records

Every AI product records each conversation as a structured lead record — CRM-style lead management built around how coaching enquiries actually work, rather than a generic contact list. Depending on the product, a record can hold:

Lead identity

Name as the lead gives it, their username, and a contact identifier or number where the channel provides one.

Qualification information

The answer to each of your questions, locked once given.

Status and state

Where the lead is: answering, qualifying, qualified, link sent, following up or handed to you.

Context and history

Relevant conversation history, so the next message — or you — has the full picture.

What you actually receive

You don't get raw access to the backend, and you don't need it. By default you receive the useful part: the lead's username, relevant chat logs, a concise lead summary, the useful lead details and a contact number where applicable. More specific lead data can be provided where appropriate. On Business, WhatsApp Personal Assistant and Fusion Max, lead data is also cleaned before it's recorded and kept in a flat view that can be sorted and exported.

Lead data is cleared from the active system on a regular cycle. The Privacy Policy explains how retention works.

Voice note handling

Plenty of fitness leads would rather talk than type. On Business and Fusion Max, a voice note is handled like any typed message:

  1. Voice noteThe lead sends audio instead of text.
  2. TranscriptionThe audio is converted to text.
  3. UnderstandingThe words are read in the context of the conversation.
  4. Text replyThe agent answers in text — and a qualifying answer counts.

A voice note can answer a qualification question just like a typed reply. The agent replies in text and doesn't send generated voice messages. Voice handling isn't included in Basic, Pro, WhatsApp Personal Assistant or Fusion. If voice matters to your audience, see Business and Fusion Max.

Pro · Business · WhatsApp PA · Fusion · Fusion Max

Follow-ups: template and AI-written

Most leads don't decide in one sitting. Qualifying products run configured follow-up sequences for leads who go quiet, matched to where the conversation stopped. There are two kinds:

Template follow-ups

Pro, WhatsApp Personal Assistant, Fusion. Messages you approve in advance. The system sends the one that matches the lead's stage.

AI-written follow-ups

Business, Fusion Max. Written fresh for each lead, referencing what that person actually said. These replace template follow-ups rather than running alongside them.

Whichever type runs, the same rules apply:

  • The moment a lead replies, books or is handed to you, anything queued for them is cancelled.
  • No follow-up is scheduled while the lead is still actively writing.
  • Timing follows the sequence configured for you and stays within each platform's messaging-window rules. WhatsApp's 24-hour window is the main constraint.

Basic doesn't send follow-ups.

Human handoff

When a lead asks to speak to a real person, the agent stops replying in that conversation and leaves it with you. It also cancels any follow-ups it had queued for that lead, so nobody gets an automated nudge in the middle of a conversation with you. The agent doesn't resume that thread on its own.

This is where a lot of automation goes wrong, which is why every AI product has it and every build is tested for it before launch.

Booking-link transfer

Transfer is the final stage of F.I.T. The booking destination you provide — a scheduling link, a form, or your own WhatsApp — goes out once, and only after a lead qualifies. Anyone who already has the link isn't sent it again.

To be precise about what this is: the system sends your link. It doesn't read or write your calendar, check real-time availability or reserve slots. The link itself handles availability. On Fusion and Fusion Max, a qualified Instagram lead can instead be moved to WhatsApp to complete booking, configured to how you work.

Pro · Business · WhatsApp PA · Fusion · Fusion Max

Hot lead alerts

A qualified lead who has just received your booking link is the warmest lead you have. Hot lead alerts make sure you know about them straight away:

  1. Lead qualifiesYour qualification is complete.
  2. Link providedYour booking link has been sent.
  3. Marked hotThe lead is treated as a hot lead.
  4. Email alertYou get an email with the lead's details.

The alert contains structured lead information, a short summary of the lead and the useful context from the conversation, so you can decide whether to reach out personally. It goes to the email address you choose during onboarding, and that address can be changed.

A hot lead alert isn't a booking confirmation. The system hasn't checked your calendar, and a lead isn't recorded as booked just because the link was sent.
