What the agent actually does

Configured once around your business, then it runs the conversation on its own.

Answers from your knowledge, not from guesses

Your programs, your pricing, your policies, your tone. If it hasn't been given an answer, it says the coach will confirm — it doesn't invent one.

Qualifies one question at a time

Goal, timeline, readiness — the questions you choose, asked in your order, one per message. Once someone answers, they're never asked again.

Follows up without being annoying

Leads who go quiet get a nudge matched to where the conversation stopped. The moment they reply or book, the queued follow-ups cancel themselves.

Sends your booking link once

Only after a lead qualifies, and only one time. No repeated links, no pestering someone who already booked.

Steps aside when you're needed

If a lead asks for a real person, the agent stops replying to that conversation and leaves it to you — and cancels any follow-ups it had queued.

Turns comments into conversations

Comment → DM is included with every Instagram product. Someone comments your keyword, they get a public reply and a DM that opens the conversation.

Our proprietary framework

The F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework

Every qualification-capable system we build follows the same three stages — designed around how coaching clients actually decide to buy. It is the reason a system built here behaves differently from a generic assistant pointed at your inbox.

Developed specifically for fitness coaching, where leads have usually failed before and are deciding whether to believe it will be different this time.

F — Filter

Qualification questions you choose separate serious buyers from browsers before either of you spends time on a call.

I — Influence

While qualifying, the conversation answers real objections using your own words, your pricing and your programs.

T — Transfer

Your booking link goes out once, only to leads who qualified — so the calls you take are with people already close to buying.

Applies to Pro, Business, WhatsApp Personal Assistant, Fusion and Fusion Max. Basic answers enquiries but does not run the full journey.
