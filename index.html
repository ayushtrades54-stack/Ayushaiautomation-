Introducing the F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework™

Every system we build for a fitness coach follows the same three-stage framework — designed specifically around how coaches sell, how their audience thinks, and what separates a buyer from a browser.

🔍

F — Filter

The system immediately separates serious buyers from freebie seekers. It asks smart qualifying questions — goals, timeline, readiness — and tags every lead automatically. You only see people who are genuinely ready to invest in themselves.

💡

I — Influence

While qualifying, the system builds trust in your offer. It handles common objections — price resistance, "I'll think about it," timing hesitation — using language built around your specific coaching style and audience.

📅

T — Transfer

Only after a lead has been filtered and warmed up does the system send your booking link. Every call on your calendar comes from someone who already knows your offer, has been pre-qualified, and wants to move forward.
