After the call, if it makes sense for both of us:

1

Deep Coaching & Profile Analysis

First 1–2 Hours After Access

Before writing a single message, I study everything about you:

  • 📱 Your Instagram profile, content style, and tone of voice
  • 🎯 Your coaching offer, audience type, and transformation promise
  • 💬 How your current DMs look and where leads are dropping off
  • ❓ What questions your audience typically asks

This is what separates a generic bot from a system that sounds like you — and converts like a sales machine.

2

Flow Architecture & Structure

Building the Skeleton Before the Words

I map out the complete automation structure before a single message is written:

  • 🔀 Every entry point — DMs, comments, stories, keywords
  • 🌿 Every decision branch — serious lead vs. not ready
  • 🏁 Every exit point — booking link, follow-up, re-engagement

Nothing gets written until the structure is locked and logical. This prevents rebuilds and wasted time.

3

Copywriting Every Single Message

Words That Qualify, Build Trust & Convert

Every message in your flow gets written from scratch — customized to:

  • 🗣️ Your coaching voice and tone
  • 🎯 Your specific audience's language and mindset
  • 🛡️ Objections your clients typically throw
  • 🔍 Smart questions that filter serious buyers from time-wasters

No copy-paste templates. Every word is built specifically for your account and your audience.

4

Full System Build in ManyChat

Turning Structure Into a Live System

All flows, triggers, conditions, tags, and sequences get built inside your ManyChat account:

  • ⚡ Keyword triggers for every entry point
  • ❓ Qualification question sequences
  • 🏷️ Lead tagging — serious / not ready / hot lead
  • 🔁 Follow-up timing sequences
  • 📅 Booking link delivery logic
  • 💬 Fallback messages for unexpected replies

💡 Throughout the build — I message you directly if I need any small inputs about your coaching. You're never left wondering what's happening.

5

Full Testing Before Going Live

Nothing Goes Live Until It's Perfect

I test every single flow personally before you see it:

  • ✅ Every trigger fires correctly
  • ✅ Every branch leads to the right message
  • ✅ Every tag applies at the right moment
  • ✅ Booking link delivers exactly when it should
  • ✅ Follow-ups fire at correct timing intervals

Only after every single path is tested and confirmed — we move to launch.

6

🚀 System Goes Live

Your Automation Is Now Running 24/7

From this exact moment:

  • ⚡ Every DM gets an instant reply — under 30 seconds
  • 🔍 Every lead gets qualified automatically
  • 📅 Every serious prospect gets pushed toward booking
  • 🔁 Every cold lead gets followed up — without you touching anything

You get a full handover message — what's live, what each part does, and what to expect going forward.

7

24-Hour Active Monitoring

I Watch It So You Don't Have To

For the first 24 hours after going live, I actively monitor:

  • 👁️ How real leads are responding in the actual flow
  • ⚙️ Whether triggers are firing correctly in real conditions
  • 💬 Any unexpected messages the system doesn't handle
  • 📉 Drop-off points in the live flow

If anything needs fixing — it gets fixed immediately. Same day. No waiting.
