Not a slideshow of features. Real conversations, showing how each product handles an enquiry, works out whether someone is serious, and what happens next.

What happens inside the demo

Every demo follows a real lead from first message to booked call, so you can judge the system on behaviour rather than a feature list.

A lead arrives

From a comment on a reel, a DM, or a WhatsApp message. Watch how fast the first reply lands and whether it sounds like the coach or like a bot.

It answers from real knowledge

Pricing, program contents, how coaching works. Notice that it never invents an answer — when it doesn't have one, it says the coach will confirm.

Qualification begins

It asks permission once, then works through the coach's questions one at a time. Watch what happens when the lead answers messily or out of order.

The booking link goes out

Only after the lead qualifies, and only once. Watch that it never re-sends the link to someone who already booked.

A quiet lead gets followed up

A second lead stalls mid-conversation. See the follow-up land — and see it cancel itself the moment they reply.

Someone asks for a human

The agent steps aside, stops replying in that thread, and cancels anything it had queued. This is the moment most automation gets wrong.

What to judge it on

  • Does it answer from real business knowledge, or generic filler?
  • One question at a time, or a wall of text?
  • Does it ever re-ask something already answered?
  • When does the booking link appear, and how often?
  • What happens when a lead asks for a real person?
  • Does the follow-up read like a human wrote it?
