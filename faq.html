The Most Important Questions First

Is this safe for my Instagram account?

Yes. The system connects through the official Meta/Instagram permission screen — you never share your password, and the connection is the same type used by any verified business tool. The automation only replies when someone messages or interacts with you first — it never cold-messages people or does anything that violates Instagram's terms. You can disconnect it anytime from your account settings. Your account remains fully in your control throughout.

Will clients know it's automated?

No. The conversation is written to feel natural and human — it sounds like you. When a lead reaches a point where personal judgment is needed, the chat is handed over to you seamlessly. The 90%+ of conversations that involve common questions, qualifications, and follow-ups are handled automatically. The real, nuanced conversations — where you're the only one who can close — come to you directly.

What if someone asks something the bot can't answer?

You'll get notified immediately and can jump into the conversation yourself. Our flows handle over 90% of common fitness coaching questions automatically — pricing enquiries, program details, goal clarifications, timing objections. Edge cases are flagged to you in real time, so you never miss an important conversation. You're never fully out of the loop.

Can I still reply manually at any time?

Absolutely. You can jump into any conversation whenever you want — and the system will pause automatically when it detects you've responded. You're always in control. The automation handles the volume; you handle the relationships that need you personally.

How quickly will I see results?

Most coaches see the system working within the first 24–48 hours of going live — leads being replied to instantly, qualification questions firing, and follow-ups happening automatically. Booked calls typically start appearing in the first week, depending on your existing DM volume and content activity. The more you post and the more leads you're getting, the faster the impact.

What's your guarantee?

If your automation doesn't generate qualified leads in the first 14 days, I rebuild the entire system from scratch — no extra charge, no questions asked. You carry zero financial risk getting started. I stand behind the work completely.

About the System & Features

What does "DM Automation Setup" actually mean?

It means I build a complete conversation flow inside your Instagram DMs (and/or WhatsApp) that activates automatically when someone messages you, comments on your post, or replies to your story. The system sends the right messages in the right sequence — qualifying the lead, building interest, handling common questions, and eventually sending your booking link to the right people. You don't touch it day-to-day.

What is Lead Qualification?

The system asks smart questions to understand if someone is a serious buyer or just browsing — their goal (fat loss, muscle gain, transformation), when they want to start, what's been holding them back, and whether they're ready to invest in a coaching program. Based on their answers, it tags them and decides whether to send your booking link or continue nurturing. You only ever receive leads who have already confirmed their interest.

What is Comment-to-DM Automation?

When someone comments on your post or reel, the system automatically sends them a personalised DM and starts a qualifying conversation. Your content stops being just for reach and starts generating real leads — every reel becomes a lead capture tool. Most coaches who add this see an immediate jump in lead volume from their existing content.

What is Story Reply Automation?

When someone reacts to or replies to your story, the system immediately engages them with a relevant, personalised message and starts the qualification process. Story reactions are high-intent signals — these people already know you and like your content. Most coaches miss these entirely because they come in sporadically. This catches every single one.

What is the Hot Lead Alert System?

When someone enters the funnel showing strong signals of high intent — quick replies, clear goal, confirmed readiness — you get an instant notification on your phone. You can jump in personally and close them while they're at peak interest. The hottest leads don't need the full automated sequence — they just need you to show up quickly. This makes sure you do.

What is the Follow-Up & Re-Engagement System?

Leads who go quiet don't just disappear — they get followed up automatically at 1 hour, 24 hours, and 3 days after their last interaction, each with a slightly different message angle. Separately, the re-engagement sequence contacts older, cold leads periodically to restart conversations. Combined, these two features typically recover 2–4 lost leads per month that would have been gone without them.

Does this work for both organic content and paid ads?

Yes. The system works for any lead entry point — organic reels and posts, stories, ad traffic, keyword DMs, and any Instagram interaction. If you're running ads, the system qualifies those leads automatically too, which significantly improves your ad ROI by making sure no paid lead ever goes unanswered.

Does this work for online coaches and gym-based coaches?

Yes, for both. The qualifying questions and flow logic get customised to your specific situation — whether you sell online transformation programs, 1-1 coaching sessions, gym memberships, or group programs. Nothing is copy-pasted from another account. Everything is built around how your specific audience makes buying decisions.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. You don't need to understand how ManyChat works, how to build flows, or anything technical. You show up to one call, fill one onboarding form, give me access — and I handle everything. After launch, you don't manage the system at all. You just reply to qualified leads and take calls.

Why not just hire a VA to do this manually?

A VA works certain hours, misses messages when they're offline, gets tired and makes mistakes, and can't respond in under 60 seconds at 2am when a lead is at peak interest. Automation replies instantly — always, without breaks, without forgetting follow-ups, and without needing to be managed daily. A VA also costs more per month than this system and delivers less consistency. The combination of speed and persistence is what gets leads to book calls.

How long does setup take?

Instagram automation: 24–72 hours after onboarding. WhatsApp or Combo setups: 48–72 hours. Everything is built, tested, and monitored for the first 24 hours after going live before being handed over to you fully.
