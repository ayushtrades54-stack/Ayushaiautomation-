A structured, rule-based system built on an established automation platform. It follows predefined paths: a keyword or button press triggers a specific reply, a tag, or a next step — the same way, every time. It doesn't read or interpret free-form messages the way an AI agent does; it matches what it's given against the rules it was configured with.

It's built and managed for you, the same as the AI products — you're not left configuring a third-party tool yourself.

Managed Automation vs AI Agents

Two real categories, doing different jobs at different prices.

Managed Automation follows predefined paths. Keywords, buttons and rules decide what happens next. Given a message it recognises, it responds perfectly and consistently. Given something unexpected, it falls back to a catch-all.

AI Agent Automation reads what the person actually wrote and responds from your business knowledge. It tracks what's been covered, understands a messy answer, and adapts to a conversation that doesn't follow a script.

Neither is universally better. If most of your enquiries are the same five questions, Managed handles them at a fraction of the cost. If your leads write in full sentences and ask things you can't fully anticipate, the AI agent handles that far better.

Managed Automation is built on established automation platforms such as ManyChat, and that's not a criticism — it's a capable, widely used tool and the right choice for structured, rule-based flows. Plenty of coaches are well served by exactly that.

AI Agent Automation is a different kind of system: a managed agent configured around your business knowledge, qualification process and tone, running on infrastructure operated and maintained as an ongoing service. The difference is architectural, not a claim that one tool is useless.

The platform subscription for Managed Automation is paid directly to that platform and is separate from the service fee. The cost is confirmed with you before setup, so there are no surprises.

We don't simply sell the platform. What you pay us for is designing, building and managing the conversation system around it. Ayush AI Automation vs ManyChat.

For AI Agent Automation there is no separate platform subscription: the AI usage and server infrastructure are covered by your monthly fee.

Because there's an ongoing cost to running it. Every conversation consumes AI usage, and the system runs on hosting and infrastructure that has to be operated, monitored and maintained continuously. Managed Automation doesn't carry that same ongoing cost structure.
