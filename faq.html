Plans' Features FAQs

What does “DM Automation Setup” mean?

We create automatic replies in your Instagram DMs so you don’t have to answer the same questions repeatedly.

What is Lead Qualification?

The system asks questions to understand if the person is serious, their goal, and when they want to start — so only real buyers reach you.

What is Tagging System?

People automatically get labels like fat loss, muscle gain, beginner, or serious — so you know who to focus on.

What is Follow-up Automation?

If someone stops replying, the system sends reminders automatically so you don’t lose leads.

What is Multi Follow-up?

Instead of one reminder, multiple reminders are sent at different times to increase replies.

What is Comment-to-DM Automation?

When someone comments on your post, they automatically receive a DM and a conversation starts.

What is Story Reply Automation?

If someone reacts or replies to your story, the system starts a conversation and turns them into a lead.

What is Call Booking Funnel?

The system guides interested people step-by-step and sends only ready clients to book a call with you.

What is WhatsApp Qualification?

Serious clients are moved to WhatsApp and filtered further so you talk only with potential buyers.

What is Re-engagement?

Old or inactive leads are contacted again so you can convert missed opportunities.

What is Hot Lead Notification?

You get alerted when a highly interested client appears so you can reply instantly and close faster.

Do I need to manage the automation daily?

No. After setup, it runs automatically. You only reply to serious leads.

Will this send spam messages?

No. It only replies to people who message or interact with you first.
