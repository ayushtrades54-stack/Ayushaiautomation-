Founder, Ayush AI Automation

Ayush AI Automation designs, builds and operates conversational automation systems for fitness coaching businesses. We work across two categories: AI Agents that hold context-aware conversations around a coach's actual programs, pricing and qualification process, and Managed Automation for coaches who need structured, rule-based flows at a lower cost.

Every system is configured around the individual coaching business rather than deployed from a template. The agency handles the entire technical side — configuration, integration, hosting, monitoring, maintenance and ongoing optimisation — so the coach is never managing infrastructure.

We work exclusively with fitness professionals: online coaches, personal trainers, gym owners, and nutrition and wellness coaches. That focus is deliberate, and it is what makes the systems behave differently from general-purpose automation.

Our proprietary framework

The F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework

The first DM automation framework designed specifically around fitness-coaching buyer psychology. Every qualification-capable system we build runs on it.

F — Filter

Qualification questions separate serious buyers from browsers before either side spends time on a call.

I — Influence

While qualifying, the conversation answers the objections coaching leads actually raise — in the coach's own words.

T — Transfer

The booking link reaches only leads who qualified, once, at the point they are warmest.

How we work

Every client works directly with the person building their system. There is no account-manager layer between the coach and the build, and no outsourced implementation — the strategy call, the configuration, the testing and the post-launch monitoring are handled by the same hands.

AI Agents rely on AI models, APIs, Meta and WhatsApp systems, hosting and other third-party services, so occasional issues, delays or platform changes can occur. We monitor, maintain and fix what is within our control, but uninterrupted, error-free operation cannot be guaranteed — and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

We are equally direct about capability. If a coach's enquiry volume doesn't yet justify an AI Agent, we say so and recommend Managed Automation or nothing at all. We do not promise guaranteed clients or revenue, because no automation system can honestly deliver that.

Why only fitness coaches

Narrow beats broad when the thing you're building depends on understanding how a specific audience decides.

The objections are consistent

Price against something they've tried before, motivation cycles, "I'll start Monday", and trust in a coach they've never met. Every coach faces the same set, which means the qualification and conversation design gets sharper with every build.

The qualification is specific

Goal, timeline, readiness, history. Generic lead qualification asks about company size and budget authority — useless for a person deciding whether to hire a coach for their own body.

The channels are the same

Instagram comments, DMs, voice notes, WhatsApp. Building deeply for two channels beats building shallowly for ten.

I can say no

Because I only work with coaches, I can tell you honestly when the answer is a cheaper Managed plan, or nothing at all right now. An agency taking any client that walks in can't afford to.
