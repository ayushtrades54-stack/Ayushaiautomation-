Built for Fitness Coaches Only

Not "works for any business." The qualification questions, the objection handling and the conversation flow are shaped around how people actually decide to hire a coach.

Why the niche matters more than it sounds

Fitness coaching has buyer psychology that almost nothing else shares. Your leads have usually failed before — a program they didn't finish, a coach who ghosted them, a gym membership they stopped using. They're not just deciding whether to buy. They're deciding whether to believe it'll be different this time.

A generic assistant answers "how much is it?" with a number. A system built for coaching knows that question is often about whether the price is worth risking on another attempt — and answers accordingly.

The conversations your DMs are actually having

These come up constantly. Your agent is configured for them during setup, using your answers.

"How much is coaching?"

Asked before they know what's included, usually as a filter. Your agent gives your real answer in your framing — and, on qualifying products, keeps the conversation going instead of letting a number end it.

"I want to lose fat, where do I start?"

A real lead, buried among fifty others. Qualification captures their goal, timeline and readiness so you can see instantly whether this is someone to call today.

"I'll start after this month"

The most expensive sentence in coaching. Their timeline gets recorded, and a follow-up reaches them later — worded for where the conversation actually stopped, not a generic "just checking in."

"I tried online coaching before, it didn't work"

Not an objection to argue with — a concern to answer honestly. Your agent uses your own words on how your coaching differs, because you know that answer better than any template does.

Voice notes instead of typing

Common in fitness DMs, especially from people who are self-conscious about writing it out. Business and Fusion Max understand voice notes and treat them exactly like typed messages.

Comments under a transformation reel

Your highest-intent moment. Comment → DM turns a keyword comment into a private conversation immediately — included with every Instagram product.

Qualification questions that fit a coaching offer

You choose your own. These are the ones coaches most often land on, and why they earn their place.

Goal

Fat loss, muscle gain, recomposition, general health. Determines whether your offer is even the right fit — and lets you open the call already knowing.

Timeline

Starting now versus "sometime soon" tells you a lot about who is ready. It also decides whether they get your booking link or a follow-up in three weeks.

Readiness to invest

Asked in your wording, not bluntly. It's what stops you spending twenty minutes on a call with someone who was never in a position to buy.

History

What they've tried and why it stopped. The most useful thing you can walk into a sales call already knowing.

Qualification is available on Pro, Business, WhatsApp Personal Assistant, Fusion and Fusion Max. Basic answers enquiries but does not qualify.

Our proprietary framework

The F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework

Built specifically for fitness coaching, because coaching leads do not buy the way other leads do. They have usually failed before, and they are deciding whether to believe it will be different this time.

A funnel that ignores that leaks people at every stage. This one is built around it.

F — Filter

Goal, timeline, readiness. Asked one at a time, in your order, in your words. A coach who takes ten calls a week to close two is spending eight hours convincing people who were never going to start. Filter fixes that before it costs you the time.

I — Influence

Qualification is also where objections get answered. "I've tried this before", "it's expensive", "I'll start next month" — each gets your real answer, in your voice, while the lead is still engaged rather than three days later when they've gone quiet.

T — Transfer

Your booking link goes only to leads who qualified, once, at the moment they are warmest. Every call on your calendar is with someone who has already told you their goal, their timeline and that they are ready.

Applies to Pro, Business, WhatsApp Personal Assistant, Fusion and Fusion Max, and to the Growth and Combo Managed plans.
