Our Proprietary Framework

The F.I.T. DM Funnel Framework™

Every system we build follows this exact three-stage logic — designed to move a stranger from their first DM to your booking calendar, automatically.

F

Filter

The first message a lead receives asks smart questions — what's their goal, when do they want to start, what's holding them back. Based on their answers, the system tags them automatically.

Freebie seekers and time-wasters are filtered out before they reach you. You only see people who have told the system they're serious and ready.

Example question the system asks: "Are you looking to start your transformation in the next 2–4 weeks, or are you still researching options?" — This one question separates buyers from browsers.

I

Influence

While qualifying, the system builds trust in your offer and handles the objections that typically kill coaching sales — "it's too expensive," "I'll think about it," "I tried before and it didn't work."

Our flows are written around the specific concerns of fat-loss clients, muscle-gain clients, and transformation seekers — not generic copy-paste scripts.

When someone says "I'll think about it," the system doesn't drop them. It sends a follow-up 24 hours later with a different angle — not pushy, just persistent in the right way.

T

Transfer

Only after a lead has been filtered and warmed up does the system send your booking link. Every call on your calendar comes from someone who already knows what you offer, has confirmed their goal, and is ready to move forward.

Your discovery calls stop being exploratory. They become closing calls — because the hard work of qualifying and building interest already happened in the DMs.

The booking link only appears when the system detects a lead is ready — not before. This keeps the conversation feeling human, not automated.
