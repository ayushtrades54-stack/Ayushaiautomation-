    What you're actually paying for

    Two fees, two different jobs. Worth understanding before you commit to anything ongoing.

    One-time setup — building it

    • Configuring the agent around your coaching business
    • Building your knowledge base — programs, pricing, policies, tone
    • Setting up your qualification questions and conversion path
    • Connecting your accounts through the official platform process
    • Testing every conversation path before launch
    • Launch preparation and handover

    Once the build is complete, this fee is non-refundable, because the work has been carried out and delivered.

    Monthly service — running it

    • AI and API management — usage for your conversations, provider access, and keeping request handling healthy
    • Managed server infrastructure — hosting and server operation for your system
    • Monitoring — watching for failures, stalls and unusual behaviour
    • Bug fixes and troubleshooting — diagnosing and repairing issues within the managed service
    • Platform and access changes — adapting when Meta, WhatsApp or a provider changes its rules, permissions or interfaces
    • Technical maintenance — keeping the system current and stable
    • Optimisation — tuning questions, answers, tone and follow-ups as your offer evolves

    AI Agent Automation is a continuously managed service, not a one-off build. If the monthly service ends, the managed system stops running, because the infrastructure, AI usage and maintenance behind it are no longer being provided.

    External costs, stated plainly

    For AI Agent Automation, the AI usage and server infrastructure needed to run your system are covered by your monthly fee.

    For Managed Automation, the automation platform subscription — for example ManyChat — is paid directly to that platform and is separate from the service fee. That is typically around $25–$30 per month depending on the plan and your contact volume, and it's confirmed with you before setup.

    Meta and WhatsApp platform requirements remain subject to their own rules and any charges they apply.

    Worth comparing properly

    Why not a cheap AI tool or a VA?

    Both are reasonable options, and for some coaches they're the right call. Here's an honest look at where each one runs out of road.

    A cheap AI reply tool

    Low-cost AI tools can generate a sensible-sounding reply to a message, and for simply acknowledging DMs that may be enough.

    WHERE THEY TEND TO STOP

    • Limited conversation context — a reply may not account for what was said three messages ago
    • Limited memory across a lead's whole journey, so questions can get repeated
    • Often no structured qualification — it answers, but doesn't work out who's serious
    • Lead details frequently aren't stored anywhere you can act on later
    • Setup, connection and upkeep are usually left to you

    A virtual assistant

    A good VA brings real human judgement, and for complex or sensitive conversations a person is genuinely better. Many coaches use both.

    PRACTICAL LIMITS

    • A recurring salary rather than a fixed service cost
    • Works set hours — the 1am DM waits until morning
    • Response speed and wording vary between people and across a shift
    • Follow-ups depend on someone remembering to send them
    • Cost scales with volume — more enquiries eventually means more hours or more people
    • Onboarding, training and turnover are your overhead
    What you get here instead

    A managed AI agent built around your business

    • Connected through the appropriate official Meta and WhatsApp Business Platform integrations
    • Richer conversation context — it tracks what a lead has already said
    • Structured qualification logic you define, not generic replies
    • Lead details and qualification answers stored as usable CRM data
    • Configuration, hosting, monitoring and maintenance handled for you
    • Cost stays predictable as your enquiry volume grows

    The agent takes the repetitive part — answering the same questions, qualifying, following up — and keeps doing it consistently. That leaves you free for the work only you can do: coaching, closing and building the business.

    Setup fee

    The one-time setup fee covers the build itself. Once the build has been completed, the setup fee is non-refundable, because the work has already been carried out and delivered.

    Monthly service & cancelling

    There is no long-term lock-in. You can cancel after any monthly cycle, and the service runs to the end of the cycle you have already paid for. When the monthly service ends, the managed AI system stops running, because the infrastructure, AI usage and maintenance behind it are no longer being provided.

    Taxes and payment

    AI Agent Automation prices are shown exclusive of GST. Any applicable taxes are added on top and confirmed with you before payment. Payment details are arranged directly on your strategy call.

    Safe connection

    Everything connects through the official Meta permission process. You never share your Instagram or WhatsApp password, you keep ownership of your accounts, and you can remove access at any time from your own settings.

    No cold messaging

    The system only responds to people who message, comment or interact with you first. It does not send unsolicited messages to strangers.
